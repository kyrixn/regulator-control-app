Valves — Arduino Giga R1

Valves 0–7 (Wire)

Valves 8–15 (Wire1)

Each bar is the muscle's length in mm from its mapped sensor (band −30…−10 mm); the label is that regulator's pressure (– when off). Enter fires that valve; APPLY ALL fires every non-empty box in one batch. Type off to turn a valve off. TIME = ramp duration in seconds (blank/0 = instant). Max 4000. Per-cell Z zeros that sensor; ZERO ALL zeros every sensor; CLEAR ZERO returns to absolute; RESCAN re-sweeps the bus and reloads the mapping.
